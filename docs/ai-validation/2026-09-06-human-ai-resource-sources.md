# 真人与AI的资源来源

Existing human 15 deduplicated historical white-player seats, including three Huanyu and three strategy company seats; compared descriptively with 24 original AI and 24 combined-scan development AI seats per company. Source totals are non-income gross gains, excluding setup, and are checked to sum exactly to nonIncomeGain. Blue1/2 credit/energy gains are reassigned using the existing owned-tech plus data-placement rule after subtracting immediate income, with an exact match to stored blue1/2 counters and a remapping ledger. Other categories remain action-context labels, not necessarily the causal reward origin; nested card/alien/industry rewards may inherit the enclosing action category. Zero raw blue source labels in structured AI logs are not zero blue-tech yield. Income gains are separately stated. Gross trade inflows are not free production and cost/settlement buckets can contain snapshot reconciliation. Human logs have missing costs/data/initial income, different versions, companies, seeds and opponents. No causal conversion rate or fresh-validation inference.
